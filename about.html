Our Story

Notre Histoire

Copain is rooted in bread, but it has grown into a family of experiences: the everyday rhythm of a neighborhood café, the precision of a pâtisserie, and the warmth of a full French brasserie.

The Concepts

One Name, Four Expressions

Copain can look a little different depending on where you visit us. The common thread is French craft, but each expression brings its own pace and purpose.

Boulangerie

Bread First

Our boulangerie is the foundation of the brand: naturally fermented loaves, baguettes, deck-oven baking, and the daily discipline of doing foundational things exceptionally well.

Café

Everyday Copain

The café expression is built for morning coffee, pastry rituals, lunch meetings, and provisions to take home. SouthPark and Winston-Salem live most closely in this rhythm.

Pâtisserie

Precision & Beauty

Pâtisserie is where detail becomes visible: laminated pastries, tarts, cakes, and delicate sweets that bring elegance to the case and a sense of occasion to the day.

Brasserie

A Full Table

Our brasserie expression expands Copain into a full-service French dining experience. At Ballantyne, that means breakfast, lunch, dinner, wine, and a hospitality style designed for lingering.

The Story

Built by Hand,
Led by Hospitality

Copain grew from Chef Jim Noble’s long belief that truly great bread changes the way a table feels. Long before the current locations, he was already baking baguettes and chasing the kind of flavor, texture, and integrity that only patience can produce.

That pursuit became Copain: a French-inspired bakery grounded in old-world technique, North Carolina ingredients, and the idea that food should feel both generous and everyday. What began as a bakery and provisions concept has continued to evolve while staying faithful to the same values.

Bread is where the story begins, but hospitality is what gives it life.
The Craft

French Method.
Carolina Sensibility.

Copain follows classic French bakery methods because they work: long fermentation, layered lamination, patient proofing, and high heat where it matters. We care about the kind of detail you can taste even if you never see the work behind it.

At the same time, Copain belongs to the Carolinas. Our ingredients, our guests, our pace of service, and our sense of welcome are all shaped by the region we call home.

By Location

Same Standard,
Different Feel

SouthPark and Winston-Salem emphasize the café-bakery side of Copain: coffee, croissants, lunch, provisions, and the daily flow of a neighborhood regular spot.

Ballantyne carries all of that forward while adding the full brasserie layer: table service, dinner, a curated wine list, and a broader sense of occasion from morning to night.
